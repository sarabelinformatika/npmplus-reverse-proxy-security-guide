# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate professionally, assume good faith, accept technical correction, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, or disclosure of confidential information.

Security disagreements should be resolved through evidence, reproducible tests, primary sources, and clearly stated assumptions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or users. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact information on the SARABEL Informatika website.
